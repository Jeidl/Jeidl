# 镜头提示词库（按功能分组，编号 A1–G4）

> Summary: 《明日不来》全部可用镜头的完整英文提示词，分为开场钩子 / 世界 / 悬疑 / 动作 / 时间特效 / 对白 / 结尾按钮七组，每条都有编号（A1–G4）、推荐模型和时长。

## 用法

- 占位符 `[STYLE-A] [NEG-A] [ADA-REF] [KEEPER-REF] [SAM-REF]` 的原文见 [风格锚](style-anchor.md) 和 [角色圣经](../concept/character-bible.md)。
- 涉及 Ada 的镜头，**同时上传 Ada 设定图作为参考图**。
- [分镜表](../production/untomorrow-shot-list.md) 用编号引用本页。
- 括号里的"模型"是建议，以 AKOOL 账号内实际可用的版本为准。

---

## A. 开场钩子（前 3 秒）

**A1 怀表倒走** · Veo / Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme macro close-up, 100mm macro lens, static camera. An antique brass pocket watch with a cracked crystal lies on black velvet, red silk ribbon curled beside it; its hands are stopped at 23:59, then the second hand twitches once and begins to sweep backward, counter-clockwise. Single hard key light, deep black background, shallow depth of field. Sound: one heavy tick, then ticking played in reverse. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**A2 睁眼** · Veo · 4 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme close-up, 85mm macro lens, static camera. The eye of [ADA-REF], lying on a pillow in cold pale-blue dawn light, snaps open instantly — fully awake and alert, no drowsiness, as if she already knows what day it is. The pupil contracts. Natural skin texture and pores. Sound: a sharp inhale, a mechanical clock flip. 4 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**A3 接住杯子** · Veo / Seedance · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Medium shot, 35mm lens, slow dolly-in. In a small dim apartment kitchen at dawn, [ADA-REF] stands reading a case file; a ceramic coffee mug slides off the counter edge and she catches it mid-fall without looking up, completely calm, as if she has done this a thousand times. An old silver transistor radio sits on the frosted windowsill. Cold pale-blue dawn light, one small warm lamp. Sound: a radio announcer says "Good morning, it's December thirty-first.", a soft whoosh, the quiet catch. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**A4 手机拍倒数 → 黎明**（社交前导用）· Kling · 8 秒
```text
Handheld smartphone footage, found-footage style, shaky framing, auto-exposure flicker, night. A joyful crowd in a snowy city square counts down loudly "Three! Two! One!" while the person filming laughs; at "One" the image glitches and smears, then hard-cuts to the same street corner in pale grey dawn light, completely empty and silent, snow untouched. Sound: cheering cut to dead silence, one distant radio voice. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

## B. 世界建立

**B1 航拍跨年城市** · Veo / Kling · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Sweeping aerial drone shot, 24mm lens, slow forward glide over a snow-covered northern city at blue hour on New Year's Eve; old stone buildings mixed with glass towers, an elevated train crossing, crowds gathering in a central square around a tall gothic clock tower; a giant blank glowing LED screen on a skyscraper. Warm sodium-amber streetlights, light snowfall. No text. Sound: distant crowd murmur, wind, a train. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**B2 空白 LED 屏**（倒计时数字后期叠加）· 任意 · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Slow push-in, 35mm lens, low angle looking up. A giant blank LED screen on the side of a dark glass skyscraper glows plain cold white over a snowy square on New Year's Eve; snow drifts through its light; tiny silhouettes of the crowd below. The screen shows no numbers and no text. Sound: low pulsing sub-bass, crowd murmur. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**B3 永不移动的指针**（延时摄影）· Kling · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Static locked-off wide shot, 50mm lens, time-lapse. The gothic clock tower above the snowy square; its giant hands point to 23:59. Around it, crowds rush past in blurred streaks, clouds race across the sky, light shifts from dusk to night — but the clock hands never move. Sound: accelerating city noise around one unchanging tick. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**B4 巨大钟楼与人群** · Veo · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme wide shot, 24mm lens, static camera, low angle. A colossal gothic clock tower rises over a snowy city square at blue hour, its enormous illuminated face glowing amber like a moon; thousands of tiny people below look up in silence; low fog drifts between them. Immense scale, perfectly still. Sound: wind, a single sustained low string note. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**B5 雪原上的人**（30 秒前导用）· Veo · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Slow aerial push-in, 24mm lens, over an endless field of untouched snow at dawn on the edge of the city; a single tiny figure — [ADA-REF] — stands alone in the middle, looking up at a pale sky where the sun refuses to rise. Vast negative space. Sound: wind, distant church bell. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**B6 欢呼人群特写**（倒数段反复使用）· Veo / Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Close-up faces of a joyful New Year's Eve crowd in a snowy square, looking up, shouting the countdown in unison, warm amber light on their faces, confetti in the air, shallow depth of field, slow motion. Bright, festive, happy — then every face freezes at the exact same instant. Sound: crowd chanting "Three! Two! One!" then a sudden hard cut to silence. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

## C. 悬疑意象

**C1 雪中尸体与怀表** · Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Low-angle close shot at ground level, 50mm lens, slow push-in, night. A body covered by a white sheet lies in fresh snow in a narrow brick alley; pulsing red and blue police lights on the wet walls; beside the protruding gloved hand, an antique brass pocket watch on a red silk ribbon lies open, cracked crystal, hands stopped at 23:59. Snowflakes settle on the glass. Sound: muffled police radio, crunching snow. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C2 证据墙** · Kling / Seedance · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Slow lateral tracking shot, 35mm lens, dark apartment at night. A wall covered floor to ceiling with hundreds of pinned photographs, city maps and red string; every photograph shows the same snowy city on the same night. [ADA-REF] stands in silhouette before it, her flashlight beam sweeping across faces circled in red marker. Sound: flashlight click, paper rustling, low drone. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C3 只剩三人的互助会** · Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Wide shot, 28mm lens, slow push-in. A dark church basement; a single bare bulb snaps on overhead, revealing a circle of mostly empty folding chairs where only three people remain seated — [ADA-REF], an elderly woman in a knitted red scarf, a nervous young man in a delivery jacket — each silently holding up an antique brass pocket watch stopped at 23:59. The bulb swings slightly. Sound: sharp switch clack, buzzing bulb, distant church bell. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C4 逆行的守时人** · Kling · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Medium-wide symmetrical shot, 40mm lens, static camera, crowded subway station. A river of commuters rides the escalator up toward the camera, while one figure — [KEEPER-REF] — rides the opposite escalator down, away from camera, perfectly still, face never visible. Cold fluorescent light, haze. Sound: footsteps, subway rumble, one ticking watch rising in the mix. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C5 钟楼表盘前的剪影** · Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Wide silhouette shot, 35mm lens, static camera. Inside a clock tower, a huge translucent illuminated clock face glows amber from behind; [KEEPER-REF] stands before it with his back to camera, perfectly still, face hidden; the giant hands point to one minute before midnight. Dust floats in the light beams. Sound: enormous slow mechanical ticking, a man's calm whisper very close to the microphone. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C6 走调的收音机** · 任意 · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Close-up, 85mm lens, slow push-in. An old silver transistor radio on a frosted windowsill at dawn, its small dial glowing; the dial light flickers as if the signal is dying. Sound: a cheerful vintage New Year's tune slows down, its pitch drops and warps into a distorted drone, then static. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**C7 戴手套的手打开怀表**（闪帧素材）· Veo · 4 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme macro close-up, 100mm lens, black background. A black leather gloved hand flips open an antique brass pocket watch on a red silk ribbon; its second hand sweeps smoothly backward; red light glints on the brass. Sound: click, reversed ticking. 4 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

## D. 动作

**D1 红光屋顶追逐** · Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Tracking shot, 35mm lens, night, heavy snowfall. [ADA-REF] sprints across a snow-covered rooftop chasing a dark figure in a long black coat; the only light is the red glow of a fading firework, painting the snow crimson; everything else falls to black. Breath steams from her mouth. Sound: pounding footsteps, wind, a muffled boom. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D2 跨楼一跃**（全片动作主镜头）· Kling / Veo · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Low-angle wide shot, 24mm lens, 120fps slow motion. [ADA-REF] leaps across the gap between two snow-covered rooftops at night, coat flaring, snow spraying from her boots, city lights far below; behind her, New Year's fireworks bloom red and gold over the clock tower. The camera tilts up as she passes overhead. Sound: wind rush, one deep heartbeat, muffled firework boom. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D3 天窗坠落 + 红色信号弹** · Kling · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Top-down shot looking straight down, 24mm lens, slow motion. [ADA-REF] falls backward through a shattered glass skylight into the dark atrium of an abandoned building, holding a burning red signal flare that trails a ribbon of crimson smoke; glass shards and snowflakes fall with her through the blackness. Sound: near silence, a dissonant wordless female choir rising. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D4 翻车**（剪辑时倒放 = 翻车复原）· Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Wide shot, 28mm lens, static camera, snowy city street at night. A black sedan swerves, clips a snowbank and flips through the air in slow motion, glass and snow exploding outward, then crashes onto its roof. Amber streetlights. Sound: tire screech, crunching metal, glass shatter. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D5 停在空中的子弹** · Veo / Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Close-up, 50mm lens, slight slow motion. [ADA-REF] fires her pistol in a snowy street at night; the bullet leaves the barrel with a flash, then stops dead in mid-air a meter in front of her, hanging motionless among frozen snowflakes, her breath vapor frozen too. Only her eyes move. Sound: gunshot cut short into total silence. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D6 螺旋楼梯** · Kling · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Low-angle handheld follow shot, 24mm lens. [ADA-REF] sprints up a narrow iron spiral staircase inside a clock tower, giant brass gears turning slowly behind her, amber light flickering through the gaps, breath ragged. The camera spirals upward with her. Sound: boots on iron, ragged breathing, a muffled crowd outside chanting a countdown. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D7 破门举枪** · Veo · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Medium shot, 50mm lens, static camera. [ADA-REF] bursts through a wooden door into the clock room and raises her pistol; across the room, backlit by the giant amber clock face, [KEEPER-REF] slowly begins to turn toward her, his face still swallowed by darkness. Sound: door slam, gun cocking, enormous ticking. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**D8 动作蒙太奇**（一次生成多镜头）· Kling 多镜头 · 10 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Multi-shot action sequence at night in a snowy city, 10 seconds total, one action per shot, consistent character and lighting.
Shot 1 (0–2s): tracking — [ADA-REF] sprints across a snowy rooftop chasing a figure in a long black coat.
Shot 2 (2–4s): low-angle slow motion — she leaps the gap between two buildings, snow spraying.
Shot 3 (4–6s): wide street — a black sedan flips through the air in slow motion.
Shot 4 (6–8s): close-up — she fires her pistol; the bullet freezes in mid-air among the snowflakes.
Shot 5 (8–10s): wide — fireworks burst over the clock tower.
Sound: heavy percussion hit on every cut, glass and metal impacts. [NEG-A]
```

## E. 时间循环特效

**E1 冻结的人群**（静默 #1）· Veo · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Slow-motion steadicam tracking shot from behind, 35mm lens. [ADA-REF] walks alone through a dense New Year's Eve crowd frozen completely still mid-cheer in a snowy square; raised arms, open mouths, confetti and snowflakes hanging motionless in the air around her; she is the only thing moving. Warm amber light, soft haze. Sound: total silence except the crunch of her footsteps. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**E2 冻结人群大远景** · Veo · 8 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme wide aerial shot, 18mm lens, slow descending crane. An endless sea of tens of thousands of people fills the snowy square, every person frozen mid-cheer like statues, confetti suspended; at the center, one tiny figure is the only thing moving, walking slowly through them. Sound: total silence, then a distant wordless choir. 8 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**E3 烟花**（剪辑时倒放 = 烟花收回火箭）· 任意 · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Wide shot, 24mm lens, static camera. Fireworks burst in a cold night sky above a gothic clock tower and a crowded snowy square, golden and red sparks blooming outward. Sound: deep firework booms, crowd cheering. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**E4 无尽通道** · Kling / Seedance · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Symmetrical one-point-perspective tracking shot, 24mm lens, moving backward in front of the subject. [ADA-REF] sprints toward the camera down an endless narrow subway tunnel lined with white tiles and cold fluorescent strips; identical wall clocks every few meters all read 23:59; far behind her at the vanishing point, [KEEPER-REF] stands perfectly still, back turned. Sound: echoing footsteps, fluorescent hum. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**E5 夜 → 晨重置转场**（首尾帧插值）· Kling / Veo 首尾帧 · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Static wide shot of the same snowy street corner. Start: night, crowded, fireworks light, confetti in the air. End: pale grey dawn, completely empty, untouched snow. The transition happens as a sudden smear of light, as if time is rewound in one breath. Sound: a reversed whoosh, then silence. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
（先生成夜景和晨景两张关键帧，再用首尾帧模式生成）
```

## F. 对白（Veo 口型同步优先；台词少于 12 个单词）

**F1 搭档对白** · Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Two-shot, 50mm lens, static camera with slight handheld breathing. In a snowy alley under pulsing police lights, [SAM-REF] crouches by a covered body and looks up at [ADA-REF], who stands above him. Sam says: "Who kills on New Year's Eve?" Ada answers flatly, without emotion: "Same guy as yesterday." Sam frowns, confused. Sound: their voices clear, ambient police radio. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**F2 Ada 独白特写** · Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Close-up, 85mm lens, slow push-in, LOC-BASEMENT lighting. [ADA-REF] looks straight past the camera, exhausted, and says quietly: "Seven of us remembered. Now there are three." Sound: her voice, buzzing bulb. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**F3 守时人耳语**：不需要画面口型。用 AKOOL 的配音工具单独生成音频，叠在 C5 或黑屏上。台词见 [字卡文案库](text-card-copy.md)。

## G. 结尾按钮

**G1 口袋里的怀表**（主按钮）· Veo · 6 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Medium close-up, 50mm lens, slow push-in, LOC-DAWN lighting. [ADA-REF] sits on the edge of her bed fully dressed in her coat; she slowly pulls an antique brass pocket watch on a red silk ribbon out of her own coat pocket; its hands are stopped at 23:59. Her expression shifts from confusion to dread. Sound: a flip clock clacks in the background, then silence. 6 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**G2 镜子慢半拍**（备选按钮，难度高）· Veo · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Medium shot, 35mm lens, static camera, bathroom mirror at dawn. [ADA-REF] lowers her hand from her face; her reflection in the mirror lowers its hand a half second later. Sound: dripping tap, a single tick. 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```

**G3 翻页钟回到 06:00** · 任意 · 4 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Extreme close-up, 100mm macro lens, static camera. A vintage black flip clock on a bedside table in a dark bedroom; its blank number cards flip over with a crisp mechanical clack. Pale blue pre-dawn light, dust in the air. No numbers or text visible. Sound: sharp mechanical flip, distant wind. 4 seconds. [NEG-A]
（数字 05:59 → 06:00 后期叠加）
```

**G4 新年歌响起的空房间** · 任意 · 5 秒
```text
[STYLE-A] Wide static shot, 28mm lens, LOC-DAWN lighting. The empty dim apartment at dawn; on the windowsill the old silver radio glows; the bed is empty, the coat is gone. Sound: the radio announcer: "Good morning, it's December thirty-first." 5 seconds. [NEG-A]
```
